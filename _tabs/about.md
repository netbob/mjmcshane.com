His core strengths lie in **C++26** and **Python**, with hands-on experience integrating the **Tesla Powerwall 3 via the Netzero API** — building monitoring, automation, and data pipeline solutions around residential energy storage.

On the personal side, he's a lifelong guitarist. ## The receipts

This site is field reports. This section is why the byline gets to write them.

**Toshiba America — 26 years, warehouse to the Solutions Lab.** I started in the warehouse and finished in charge of the Solutions Lab. In between: AS/400 systems analyst, network and email administration, corporate IT, Senior Manager of IT. The infrastructure and security posts here come from having run those systems at corporate scale, not from having read about them.

The lab, in its final form: an AS/400, a Sun Solaris server, Active Directory domain controllers, an Exchange mail server, and a VMware ESX virtual-machine environment. I also built its traveling twin — a locked airline crate carrying a complete set of Toshiba notebooks, servers, and the wireless infrastructure to run them anywhere.

**DocuWare — certified document systems engineer.** After Toshiba I consulted (as TechPointPro LLC) on document management: DocuWare installations and support, SQL and MySQL, virtualized customer environments. Paper is just a database that hasn't been scanned yet.

**The current bench, in Clovis, California.** A Tesla Powerwall 3 running a home-built automation and advisory layer (the code is public, in the powerwall-dispatch repo). A W65C02S breadboard computer — a 1985 Apple //e being rebuilt one chip at a time, documented as a series on this site. Two Jekyll sites run end to end. And a novel in serial progress at [netbob.org](https://netbob.org), because the engineering was never the only thing.
