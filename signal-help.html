Replying via Signal

On the contact page, the "How can I reply?" box takes an email address — or a Signal handle, if you'd rather I answer you on Signal. Either way it travels inside the encrypted message, so only I see it.

What you need

  1. Signal installed. Free from signal.org, for iPhone, Android, and desktop.
  2. A Signal account. Registering takes a phone number — but that number stays private. Nobody you message through Signal sees it unless you choose to share it.
  3. Optionally, a username. In Signal, go to Settings → Profile → Username and pick one. With a username, I can message you without ever learning your phone number.

What to put in the box

Label it so I know it's Signal, not email:

  • Signal: @yourusername — best for privacy.
  • Signal: +1… — your number, if you're fine with me seeing it.

What happens next

I decrypt your message, then send you a Signal message from my account. Signal messages are end-to-end encrypted — no one but the two of us can read them.

Don't have Signal? Just leave an email address instead — same box, same encryption.
